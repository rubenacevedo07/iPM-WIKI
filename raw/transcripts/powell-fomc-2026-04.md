## Current Rate Decision
- Fed held federal funds rate at ~4.5–4.75% target range (restrictive level maintained)
- SEP dot plot showed fewer cuts projected for 2026 vs December — more members see "higher for longer"
- No formal dissenting votes; press conference acknowledges internal differences on pace of future cuts

## Key Quotes (paraphrased)
- On inflation: inflation has fallen from 2022 peaks but "remains somewhat above" 2% target; PCE total ~2.8–2.9% YoY; core moderating but elevated
- On rate path: Fed "is in no hurry to cut"; rate path depends on data; majority sees cuts possible later in 2026 if disinflation continues; premature cuts could reignite inflation
- On labor market: still solid but more balanced; more moderate job growth, rising participation, decelerating wages — helping ease inflation without sharp unemployment rise
- On tariffs/trade: Fed monitors how tariffs raise imported goods prices but tries to separate one-time price shocks from underlying inflation trend; Fed mandate does not include designing trade policy

## Narrative Shift vs Prior Meeting
- Greater emphasis that inflation remains ABOVE target; upward revisions to 2026 inflation projections (2.7% vs 2.5% prior) reduce space for aggressive cuts
- Somewhat more hawkish tone: fewer references to downside activity risks, more to inflation re-acceleration risk if energy and tariffs press prices again
- Powell frames recent energy and tariff shocks as short-term "noise" but flags Fed cannot ignore possibility they become persistent if they anchor expectations higher

## Forward Guidance
- Core message: rates will remain restrictive "as long as necessary" until greater confidence inflation will sustainably converge to 2%
- SEP still points to gradual cuts later in 2026 but with higher terminal rate than suggested months ago; "higher for longer" remains implicit narrative
- Powell avoids committing to specific date for first cut; conditions everything on inflation and activity data

## Market Impact Signals
- Rates: hawkish tone + SEP pushed short end of curve higher, some additional flattening/inversion as probability of rapid cuts reduced
- USD: appreciated vs major crosses after press conference, reflecting hawkish reading relative to other jurisdictions
- Equities: mixed; rate-sensitive sectors (growth, tech) under some pressure; banks and financials benefit from higher-for-longer scenario
