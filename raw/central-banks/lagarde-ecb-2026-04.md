## Current Rate Decision
- ECB holds three key rates unchanged; previously completed hiking cycle and small cut in 2025; current orientation is watchful pause with slightly restrictive rates

## Key Quotes (paraphrased)
- On inflation: inflation "is around our 2% medium-term target"; updated projections show similar profile to recent meetings; core still requires vigilance
- On growth: eurozone growth is weak with country heterogeneity but no generalised recession; monetary policy must not overreact to volatile short-term data
- On EUR: exchange rate is not a target but strong EUR moves affect inflation projection; avoids "desired level" comments — standard doctrine
- On fiscal policy: fiscal policy must be more targeted and less expansive; phase out broad support measures; prioritise investments that raise growth potential

## Narrative Shift vs Prior Appearance
- 2025 shift: from "further hikes possible" to "we have probably reached the peak"; 2026 narrative consolidates to "prolonged pause with vigilance" — no promise of rapid cuts
- Less emphasis on risk of unanchored inflation, more on avoiding excessive tightening that drags activity — but carefully balanced to avoid sounding dovish
- Language suggests ECB is comfortable with current restriction while verifying inflation stabilises around target

## Forward Guidance
- ECB maintains conditional formulation: will decide rate level and duration meeting by meeting based on data and transmission of monetary policy
- Door open to cuts if inflation durably below projected path and activity weakens more, but without prior commitments

## Market Impact Signals
- Fixed income: pause decision and "no more hikes" message supports moderate core bond rally; peripheral spread compression as market prices less over-tightening risk
- EUR: moderate reaction; when speech emphasises end of hiking cycle, euro tends to weaken vs USD especially if Fed maintains more hawkish tone
- European equities: relief in financing-intensive sectors; real estate still constrained by high rates; sensitivity to any signal of pivot toward cuts
