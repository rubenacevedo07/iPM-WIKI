## China
- Continuation of elevated tariffs on broad basket of Chinese goods (Section 301 heritage) with threats of new increases on sensitive technologies and strategic sectors
- 2026 investigation announced into trade practices and subsidies — could lead to new tariffs by summer 2026
- Tech decoupling narrative: export controls on advanced semiconductors remain in place and are being tightened

## European Union
- US opened investigations threatening new tariffs linked to EV, agriculture, and industrial policy disputes
- Some inherited tariffs (steel/aluminium) maintained at elevated levels with reciprocal adjustments
- Potential escalation if EU retaliates or imposes carbon border adjustment on US goods

## Other Major Partners
- New trade investigation extends to 16 countries and regions
- Door open to tariffs on broader group of partners justified by "unfair practices" and trade imbalances
- Mexico, Southeast Asia, India positioning as potential beneficiaries of supply chain rerouting (friendshoring)

## Executive Orders & Legal Instruments
- 2025-07-30: Presidential action "further modifying the reciprocal tariff rates" — adjusted tariffs on certain partners in response to their treatment of US products
- 2026-03: Announcement of trade investigations that may lead to new tariffs vs China, EU, and 14 other partners

## Narrative Shift
- Since January 2025 inauguration: from "reviewing inherited agreements" to explicitly confrontational: tariffs as central tool of industrial policy and national security
- Greater emphasis on "reciprocity" and "punishing unfair practices" — less multilateral language; broader range of countries under investigation beyond China
- Trade tariff debate directly merged with tech competition and geopolitics (chips, AI, clean energy)

## Market Impact
- USD: high Fed rates + trade tensions tend to support dollar as safe haven; extreme trade risk episodes can cause sharp moves in Asia/Europe pairs
- Affected sectors: European and Asian exporters in autos, tech, industrial goods face greater market access uncertainty; US companies with China/EU supply chains face higher costs and margin volatility
- Supply chain shifts: friendshoring/nearshoring toward Mexico, Southeast Asia, and allied countries accelerating — reconfiguring trade and investment flows
