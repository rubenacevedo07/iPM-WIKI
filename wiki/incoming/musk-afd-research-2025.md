## Musk ↔ AfD: Endorsement Timeline

### December 2024
- Musk begins posting enthusiastic endorsements of the AfD on X, calling it "the only party that can save Germany"
- Publishes an op-ed in a major German newspaper arguing for AfD support
- His initial AfD posts trigger an 8-fold increase in collective views for AfD politicians on X the week following (DFRLab/AlgorithmWatch analysis)

### January 9, 2025
- **X Space livestream with Alice Weidel** (~200,000 live listeners)
- Musk opens by introducing Weidel as "the leading candidate to run Germany" (factually contested — she had no path to chancellorship)
- During the livestream, Musk says: "People really need to get behind the AfD. Otherwise, things are going to get very much worse in Germany."
- Musk calls Weidel "a very reasonable person. Nothing outrageous is being proposed, just common sense."
- Weidel makes the claim that Hitler was "a communist, socialist guy" — not right-wing. Musk agrees.
- EU officials (European Commission) monitor the stream for potential Digital Services Act violations (hate speech, manipulation of civic discourse)

### January 25, 2025
- Musk makes a **surprise virtual appearance at a 4,500-person AfD rally** in Eastern Germany
- Tells the crowd: "fight for a great future for Germany"

### Pre-election period (Jan–Feb 2025)
- Repeated X posts urging Germans to vote AfD
- Drops endorsement of UK Reform/Nigel Farage, calling him lacking "what it takes" — AfD as his preferred European far-right vehicle at the time

### German Federal Election — February 23, 2025
- AfD finishes second with ~20% of the vote
- Despite Musk's campaigning, AfD remains blocked from coalition government; all other major parties refuse to govern with it
