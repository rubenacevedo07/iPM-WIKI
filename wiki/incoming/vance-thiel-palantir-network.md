## The Thiel → Vance Pipeline: Career Reconstruction

### First contact: Yale, 2011
- Thiel gives a talk at Yale Law School criticizing America's "technological stagnation"
- JD Vance (then law student) attends; credits Thiel with steering him away from big law toward entrepreneurship
- Thiel later blurbs *Hillbilly Elegy* and calls Vance "a good friend"

### Mithril Capital, 2016–2017
- Vance joins Mithril Capital Management (Thiel/Ajay Royan VC firm, founded 2012, named after Tolkien metal) as principal
- Hillbilly Elegy published June 2016 — makes Vance a nationally known figure during his tenure
- Former colleagues' accounts mixed: some remember good instincts, others recall rarely seeing him in office
- Leaves in spring 2017; no major deals attributed to his time there per WSJ reporting

### Revolution LLC, 2017–2019
- Joins Revolution (Steve Case's firm); co-leads 14 deals including in defense and AI sectors
- Colin Greenspon (Mithril colleague) joins him in 2018
- Better-remembered tenure at Revolution than Mithril

### Narya Capital, 2020–2022
- Co-founds Narya in Cincinnati/Ohio with Greenspon; raises $93M+
- Investors include Thiel and former Google CEO Eric Schmidt
- Backs AppHarvest (Kentucky indoor farm); board member; steps down to run for Senate
- AppHarvest goes bankrupt 2023

### The Senate Run, 2022 — Thiel's Kingmaker Moment
- 2021: Thiel accompanies Vance to Mar-a-Lago for his introduction to Trump
- Thiel donates **$15 million** to Vance's Ohio Senate campaign — the largest single-donor contribution in Senate history at the time
- Trump endorsement follows; Vance wins Ohio Senate seat
- Thiel simultaneously donates $10M to Blake Masters' (Arizona) Senate campaign

### VP Selection, July 2024
- Thiel, Musk, David Sacks, Tucker Carlson among those lobbying Trump to pick Vance
- Thiel specifically credited with first introducing Trump to Vance in 2021
- Trump selects Vance as VP candidate; Musk responds: "Resounds with victory"

### VP Inauguration, January 20, 2025
- Vance becomes 49th/50th Vice President of the United States
- Thiel's influence now operates "less through his own presence than through a network of over a dozen allies" in the executive branch (Revolving Door Project)
