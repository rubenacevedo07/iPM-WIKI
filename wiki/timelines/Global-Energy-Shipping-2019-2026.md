# Global Energy & Shipping Timeline (2019–2026)

*Covers: wars, sanctions, chokepoints, supply shocks, price movements.*

---

## 2019–2021: Pre-War & Early Tensions

- **2019-09:** Drone attacks on Saudi Aramco Abqaiq facility — single largest oil supply disruption in history (~5% global supply temporarily removed); Houthi/Iran attributed
- **2019-09:** Tanker seizures in Gulf of Oman begin — Iran detains UK-flagged Stena Impero; Hormuz tension escalates
- **2020-03:** COVID-19 → oil demand collapse → Brent briefly negative ($-37/barrel); OPEC+ emergency cuts
- **2020-04:** OPEC+ historic production cut (9.7M b/d) — MBS/Putin coordination to stabilise market
- **2021:** Energy demand recovery faster than supply response → price surge begins

---

## 2022–2024: Ukraine War & Russia Sanctions

- **2022-02-24:** Russia invades Ukraine → European energy dependency exposed; natural gas prices spike 10x; Germany declares energy emergency
- **2022-03:** EU begins emergency LNG import surge from US, Qatar, Norway to replace Russian pipeline gas
- **2022-06:** Brent peaks ~$120/barrel on supply disruption fears
- **2022-12-05:** G7/EU oil price cap introduced at $60/barrel for Russian crude — seaborne exports targeted
- **2023:** Shadow fleet operational at scale — estimated 400-600 tankers operating outside Western insurance/financial system
- **2023:** Urals discount to Brent stabilises at $10-20/barrel — Russia adapts exports to India/China
- **2023-Q4:** Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping begin following Hamas-Israel war outbreak
- **2024-Q1:** Major shipping companies (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM) reroute all Asia-Europe traffic via Cape of Good Hope
- **2024:** Western enforcement tightened — entity list additions for shadow fleet operators; insurance blacklisting

---

## 2024–2025: Red Sea / Houthi Disruption

- **2024-01:** Bab el-Mandeb threat level elevated to 7/10 — sustained Houthi missile and drone campaign
- **2024-02:** US-UK Operation Prosperity Guardian — naval escorts and strikes on Houthi positions; limited effectiveness
- **2024-Q2:** Baltic Dirty Tanker Index elevated — Cape rerouting adding $1-2M per voyage
- **2024-Q3:** Lloyd's of London declares Red Sea a permanent war risk zone — insurance premiums +200-400% on prior baseline
- **2025:** India begins reducing Russian oil purchases as discounts narrow — from ~1.2M b/d toward ~0.5M b/d
- **2025-Q4:** G7/EU adjust price cap implementation guidelines — more documentation required; enforcement tightened

---

## 2026: Current Status

- **2026-03:** Western sanctions enforcement further tightened — cargo inspections, shadow fleet blacklisting intensified
- **2026-04:** Bab el-Mandeb at **8/10** — highest active chokepoint risk in dataset (surpassing Taiwan Strait)
- **2026-04:** Hormuz at **7/10** — elevated but no active blockade; IRGC incident risk remains
- **2026-04:** Urals discount to Brent persists; Russia oil revenue under pressure from India purchase reduction
- **Pending:** Iran escalation scenario — if nuclear negotiations fail, Hormuz threat level could spike to 9-10
