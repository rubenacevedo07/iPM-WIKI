# AI & Tech-Decoupling Timeline (2019–2026)

*Covers: export controls, fab investments, AI model launches, supply chain shifts.*
*Append-only — add new events at the bottom of each section.*

---

## 2019–2021: Early Controls

- **2019-05:** Huawei added to US Entity List → TSMC, Google, ARM cut ties → first major tech decoupling signal
- **2019-10:** Additional Chinese AI and surveillance companies added to Entity List (Hikvision, SenseTime, etc.)
- **2020-09:** TSMC cuts all Huawei chip supply following expanded US rules requiring license for any foreign company using US technology
- **2020:** Huawei HiSilicon loses ability to tape out at TSMC — Kirin chip production ends
- **2021:** SMIC (China's largest foundry) added to Entity List — cannot access US equipment for leading-edge nodes

---

## 2022–2023: Advanced Chip Controls

- **2022-08:** CHIPS and Science Act signed — $52B for US semiconductor manufacturing; TSMC, Samsung, Intel incentivised to build US fabs
- **2022-10-07:** BIS sweeping export controls — restrict A100/H100 GPU exports to China; extend to semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) at 14nm and below; requires license for US persons to support Chinese chipmakers
- **2023-01:** Netherlands agrees to restrict ASML EUV shipments to China following US diplomatic pressure
- **2023-03:** Japan agrees to join export controls on 23 categories of advanced semiconductor equipment
- **2023-07:** NVIDIA launches A800/H800 "China versions" — reduced compute to avoid controls
- **2023-10:** BIS closes A800/H800 loophole — new rules restrict chips above bandwidth thresholds regardless of performance
- **2023-09:** Huawei Mate 60 Pro launches with Kirin 9000S chip — apparently manufactured by SMIC at ~7nm; shocks US intelligence; demonstrates China can partially work around controls

---

## 2024–2025: Scaling AI & Fabs

- **2024:** TSMC Arizona Fab 21 Phase 1 (4nm) begins production — first leading-edge fab on US soil
- **2024:** TSMC Japan (Kumamoto) fab opens — 28nm/16nm; strategic diversification
- **2024-Q1:** NVIDIA H100 supply shortage eases as TSMC CoWoS packaging capacity expands; but HBM (SK Hynix) remains tight
- **2024-Q2:** NVIDIA Blackwell (B100/B200) architecture announced — next generation GPU; production starting H2 2024
- **2025:** Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta each announce $50B+ AI capex — structural demand for NVIDIA/TSMC/HBM supply chain
- **2025:** China "Big Fund III" — estimated ¥344B ($47B) semiconductor investment fund; focus on mature nodes and equipment self-sufficiency
- **2025:** Intel struggles with 18A process — market share at risk from TSMC and Samsung on leading edge
- **2025-Q4:** TSMC Arizona Phase 2 (2nm) construction begins

---

## 2026: Tightening Controls & New Ecosystem

- **2026-01:** Trump administration reviews all semiconductor export control policies — signals potential tightening
- **2026-03:** Trade investigation announced covering China and 14 other countries — tech and semiconductor sectors included
- **2026-Q1:** ASML reports backlog at record levels; High-NA EUV (next generation sub-2nm) first customer shipments
- **2026-04:** China still cannot access EUV — running 2-3 process generations behind TSMC; gap not closing
- **Pending:** New BIS rules expected mid-2026 — likely to further restrict advanced packaging exports to China
