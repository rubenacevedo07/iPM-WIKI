# Global Macro Regime — April 2026

## Current Narrative
"Moderate but resilient growth with stubborn disinflation and elevated geopolitical fragmentation."

The IMF projects 3.3% global growth for 2026. The US and India are the primary growth engines. The eurozone is weak but not recessionary. China is slowing structurally. Inflation is falling but unevenly — central banks are not yet done.

## Historical Baseline
2022–2023: aggressive global tightening cycle to fight post-COVID inflation surge.
2024–2025: disinflation progress; ECB and some EMs began cutting; Fed held.
2026: "higher for longer" becomes the dominant regime. Cuts delayed further. Tariff shocks threatening re-acceleration.

## Recent Shifts (as of April 2026)
- **Fed:** hawkish pivot within the pause — dot plot revised to fewer 2026 cuts, inflation projections revised up to 2.7%
- **ECB:** holding at slightly restrictive levels; watchful pause; weak eurozone growth limits further tightening
- **IMF:** global growth slightly revised up but fragmentation risk escalating; fiscal consolidation urgently needed in US
- **Trade:** Trump tariff investigations expanding to 16+ countries — fragmentation accelerating

## Carriers
- **Powell:** "higher for longer, data dependent, no hurry to cut"
- **Lagarde:** "watchful pause, meeting by meeting, no pre-commitment"
- **IMF:** "moderate resilient growth, credible fiscal consolidation needed, multilateral cooperation or fragmentation damage"
- **Trump:** "tariffs as industrial policy, reciprocity, America-first"

## Conflicting Narratives
- **Soft landing vs stagflation:** Fed holding while tariffs push up prices = stagflation risk if labor market weakens simultaneously
- **Dollar strength vs de-dollarization:** USD bid from high rates but structural erosion from fragmentation, BRICS activity, gold accumulation
- **AI productivity boom vs fragmentation loss:** Tech sector betting on AI productivity offsetting tariff and supply chain inefficiency

## Market Read-Through
- **Rates:** short end elevated globally; long end uncertain — fiscal deficits vs disinflation
- **FX:** USD structurally bid; EUR structurally pressured; EM FX vulnerable
- **Equities:** US outperforms ex-US; value over growth; defensives + AI infrastructure = outliers
- **Commodities:** oil range-bound (supply control vs demand softness); gold bid on uncertainty
