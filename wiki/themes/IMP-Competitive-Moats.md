# IMP Competitive Moats — 17 Moats, 5 Categories

## Summary
17 moats across 5 categories. The social network moat is the most important — it separates a product from a platform.

## Category 1 — Social Network (the compounding moat)
- **Analyst Score as credential:** log-weighted accuracy on resolved predictions. Once analysts have a score they don't leave — it's their professional identity. Switching cost compounds with every resolved prediction.
- **Map forking with attribution chain:** fork chain carries attribution permanently. Intellectual contribution compounds.
- **Community bets build the prediction market:** every TimelineBet shifts branch probability. The crowd builds the market.
- **Graph enrichment by community:** every connection drawn, every news event anchored adds to the graph. This data cannot be bought.

## Category 2 — Data & Signal Moats

| Moat | Why It's Irreplaceable |
|---|---|
| **Causal graph — 1,230+ edges** | 18 months of curation. Mechanism not correlation. Exact entity path from event to asset. |
| **EdgeRiskScore** | SeverityLevel × BranchProbability (0-5). Updated live every 5 minutes from Polymarket CLOB. No competitor has this. |
| **Three-way prediction signal** | Human Polymarket vs AI ensemble vs individual machine. Divergence >20pts = alpha. Cannot be manufactured without Oracle. |
| **Prediction dataset** | After 24 months: labeled graph state + probability trajectory + outcome + edge changes. Cannot be manufactured retroactively. |
| **Oracle calibration data** | Regime-conditioned accuracy per machine per domain. Only accumulated through time. |
| **Fine-tuning dataset** | After 24 months: licensable training data for financial AI models. |
| **GCRI/DSI/WPI/GPI** | Graph-propagated composite indexes. Categorically different from keyword-counting GPR. |
| **PersonPowerIndex** | No public product ranks individuals by composite power with measurable reach. Once the ranking exists, it becomes the reference. |
| **Chokepoint intelligence** | 7 geographic control points with closure risk, GDP impact, controller military capacity. Sub-10-minute intelligence advantage. |

## Category 3 — Retention & Lock-in Moats
- Analyst Score credential: verifiable accuracy tied to on-chain Polymarket P&L
- Intellectual work product: custom PowerMaps cannot be exported to Bloomberg or rebuilt in TradingView
- ResolutionImpactLog: immutable audit trail per resolution. Regulatory compliance value compounds.
- Strategy Lab workspaces: saved strategies conditioned on IMP graph state cannot run anywhere else — QuantConnect doesn't have the graph

## Category 4 — Distribution & Growth Moats
- **3 viral loops:**
  1. Prediction screenshot on X
  2. Map explains breaking news → 50K views in 48h
  3. Due diligence report shared at PE firm → 10 enterprise inquiries from one document
- **SEO compounding:** 500+ entity pages at launch. 'NVIDIA geopolitical risk' ranks. Every new entity = new indexed page.
- **Builder fees:** 0.5% passive Polymarket revenue on every CLOB order. Scales automatically.
- **Network effects:** more analysts → more accurate predictions → more users → more bets → more data → better AI → stronger signals → higher retention → more analysts

## Category 5 — Lab & Observatory Moats
- **Butterfly Effect Engine:** remove any decision node, watch the cascade. No competitor models systemic decision cascades visually.
- **Invisible Hand Tracker:** follow any war, bailout, or rate hike to its true beneficiaries through ownership and capital flow edges.
- **Wall of Peace:** model structural cost of continued conflict vs negotiated resolution.
- **Sustainable World Engine:** compute consequences of production decisions on human wellbeing.
