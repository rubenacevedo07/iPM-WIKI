# Dollar System & Fed Liquidity

## Strategic Summary

The dollar system is the invisible infrastructure of the IMP graph. Every edge that involves financing, trade, or coercive pressure runs through dollar-denominated settlement. The Fed is the central node — it sets the price of dollars globally via the federal funds rate, and its balance sheet determines how many dollars exist in the system. Wall Street is the distribution mechanism. The Pentagon is the ultimate enforcement arm.

This theme connects three IMP indicators:
- **[[../indicators/Fed-Liquidity-Pulse]]** — is the Fed injecting or draining?
- **[[../indicators/USD-Hegemony-Index]]** — is dollar dominance intact or eroding?
- **[[../indicators/US-Military-Projection]]** — is the military backstop credible?

## The Dollar Power Stack

```
Pentagon (coercive backstop)
    ↓ guarantees
US Treasury (debt issuance, sanctions)
    ↓ finances
Federal Reserve (monetary base, interest rates)
    ↓ sets cost of
Wall Street (capital allocation, risk pricing)
    ↓ flows into
Global Dollar System (trade, reserves, debt)
```

Every node in the IMP graph that needs dollar funding is structurally dependent on this stack. When it is strong, US power projection is near-unlimited. When any layer weakens, alternatives accelerate.

## Fed Balance Sheet as Power Instrument

The Fed's $7T balance sheet is not just a monetary tool — it is a geopolitical instrument:

**QE (expansion):**
- Dollars flood global system → EM capital inflows → US allies get cheap funding
- Commodity prices rise (dollar weakness) → oil exporters benefit
- Risk assets rise globally → wealth effect in allied economies

**QT (contraction):**
- Dollars drain from global system → EM capital outflows → dollar funding stress
- Rising real rates → indebted governments stressed
- Commodity prices fall (dollar strength) → oil exporters hurt
- Used as implicit pressure on adversaries dependent on dollar financing

**Current (April 2026):** QT ongoing. Fed assets ~$7T (down from $9T peak). Real rates ~2.5%. Net contracting → global dollar tightening. See [[../indicators/Fed-Liquidity-Pulse]].

## Dollar as Sanctions Weapon

Post-2022 Russia sanctions, the dollar's weaponisation accelerated:
- Russia excluded from SWIFT → $300B sovereign reserves frozen
- Iran oil revenues blocked → shadow fleet workaround
- China watching: accelerating CNY internationalisation, gold accumulation

Trump GeoScore = −4.0 (isolationist). Bessent EconScore = 6.0 (fiscal conservative). The combination creates a specific risk: tariffs + sanctions escalation → more dollar weaponisation → faster de-dollarization by adversaries → USD Hegemony Index declines faster.

**The paradox:** using the dollar as a weapon accelerates the erosion of its power.

## Three Scenarios for the Dollar System

**Scenario A — Gradual erosion (baseline):**
USD reserves share continues declining ~0.5-1% per year. Dollar dominant but less so. Multipolar currency system emerges over 10-20 years. Bitcoin and gold capture marginal reserve diversification.

**Scenario B — Dollar crisis (tail risk):**
US fiscal crisis (debt downgrade, bond market strike) + Fed loses credibility + geopolitical shock simultaneously. Dollar falls sharply. IMP Bitcoin Regime C activates. Gold spikes. EM debt crisis.

**Scenario C — Dollar reinvigoration:**
US fiscal consolidation (Bessent 3-3-3), Fed credibility restored via successful soft landing, energy independence deepens. Dollar remains primary reserve currency through 2040+.

**Current IMP assessment: Scenario A with Scenario B tail rising.**

## Market Impact

- **USD (DXY):** Fed hawkish → USD strong. Dollar weaponisation → structural demand for alternatives long-term.
- **Gold:** Safe haven from dollar system uncertainty. Central bank accumulation (China, Russia) structural bid.
- **Bitcoin:** Regime B now (underweight). Regime C trigger = dollar crisis or capital controls.
- **US Treasuries:** Global safe haven but fiscal sustainability questions building.
- **EM assets:** Dollar tightening = EM stress. Dollar weaponisation = EM diversification away from USD.
