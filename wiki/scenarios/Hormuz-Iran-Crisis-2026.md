# Hormuz / Iran Crisis 2026 Scenarios

*Current threat level: 10/10 (highest in DB). Hormuz = ~20M barrels/day, 20% of global oil supply, 30% of global LNG. The IRGC has demonstrated willingness to seize tankers and threaten closure. Iran's nuclear programme is the primary trigger variable.*

---

## Scenario Space

**Trigger node:** Iran nuclear threshold crossed OR US/Israel strike decision
**Primary actor:** Iran IRGC (coercive instrument of Khamenei)
**Secondary actor:** MBS (OPEC+ response), Powell (inflation mandate)
**Constraint:** Oil price $150+ triggers US/EU recession — all parties know this

---

## Branch A — Hormuz Closure (Partial or Full)
*Probability: 20-25% over 12 months*

**Trigger sequence:**
1. Iran crosses 90% uranium enrichment threshold
2. Israel strikes Natanz / Fordow nuclear facilities
3. IRGC activates "Passive Defence Doctrine" — mines Hormuz, seizes tankers
4. 5th Fleet + allied naval forces attempt to clear — 2-4 week disruption minimum
5. Saudi Arabia attempts to route via East-West pipeline (capacity: 5M b/d, not enough)

**Oil price trajectory:**
- Day 1-3: Brent +$25-35 (shock premium)
- Week 1-2: Brent $130-150 (supply reality sets in)
- Month 1-3: Brent $150-180 if closure sustained (2008 levels adjusted for inflation)
- Iran Urals equivalent: zero (shadow fleet paralysed)

**Inflation cascade:**
- CPI: +1.5-2.5% in 3 months (energy pass-through)
- Powell's dilemma: hike to fight inflation OR cut for recession → **stuck**
- Fed Liquidity Pulse: forced to hold → Bitcoin Regime B extended
- EUR/USD: EUR collapses (Europe net energy importer, no US energy independence buffer)
- ECB: Lagarde faces stagflation — cannot cut without currency crisis

**IMP DB updates triggered:**
- Hormuz `ClosureRiskScore` holds at 10 — event confirmed
- Bab el-Mandeb escalates to 9 (Houthi opportunism)
- ScenarioCascade Oil $150 template ACTIVATES
- GCRI: +30-40 points (from current ~71 to 100+ territory)
- ExxonMobil, Saudi Aramco: `OilSensitivityScore` +92 = massive benefit
- Airlines: `OilSensitivityScore` -88 = structural crisis

**Market cascade:**
- Brent: +$60-80 from current
- Airlines: −50 to −70% (existential)
- Defense stocks (Lockheed, Raytheon): +30-50%
- Gold: +$300-500/oz (safe haven + inflation)
- Bitcoin: initial −20% (risk-off) then Regime C bid if dollar confidence cracks
- USD: spike then structural weakness (weaponisation accelerates de-dollarization)

**Resolution paths:**
- A1: US/Israel strikes destroy IRGC naval capability → Hormuz reopens → 60-90 days
- A2: Ceasefire negotiation → Iran JCPOA re-entry talks → 3-6 months
- A3: Sustained closure + Iran-Russia-China alliance deepens → systemic energy shock

## Branch B — Nuclear Deal (Iran re-enters JCPOA equivalent)
*Probability: 25-30%*

**Trigger:** Trump administration offers sanctions relief for verified nuclear freeze

**Oil supply effect:**
- Iran oil exports: 1.5M b/d → 3.0M+ b/d within 12 months
- Brent: −$15-25 from current (supply surge)
- Saudi Arabia: forced to cut OPEC+ output to defend $80 floor
- MBS political pressure: revenue shock

**Broader effects:**
- Hormuz threat: drops to 4/10
- Red Sea/Houthi: partial de-escalation (Iran proxy pressure reduced)
- Israel: political crisis (Netanyahu coalition collapses without Iran threat narrative)
- Russia: loses Iran as parallel sanctions-evader — Urals discount narrows

**Market cascade:**
- Oil: −$15-20
- Airlines: +20-30%
- Defense: −10-15%
- Emerging markets: rally (lower energy costs)
- USD: mild weakening (lower inflation = more Fed cuts = lower yields)

---

## Branch C — Frozen Conflict (Current Trajectory)
*Probability: 50-55% baseline*

Houthi attacks continue, Hormuz threat elevated but no closure. Oil range-bound $75-95. Fed remains hawkish. Status quo with periodic incident spikes.
