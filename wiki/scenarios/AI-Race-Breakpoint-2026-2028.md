# AI Race Breakpoint 2026–2028 Scenarios

*The AI race is the first technology competition where the outcome determines military, economic, and information dominance simultaneously. The "breakpoint" is when one side achieves a capability threshold that forces the other to respond systemically — not just commercially.*

---

## Scenario Space

**Trigger nodes:** Model capability threshold crossed (AGI-adjacent) OR China achieves parity
**Primary actors:** Jensen Huang (NVIDIA — controls the compute), Sam Altman (OpenAI — frontier lab), Xi Jinping (China AI strategy)
**Key constraint:** Export controls = US chokehold on China's AI progress. But every year that passes, China closes the gap on non-frontier applications.

---

## Branch A — US Frontier Lab Achieves AGI-Adjacent Capability
*Probability: 20-25% by end 2027*

**Definition:** A model demonstrably automates >50% of white-collar cognitive work with superhuman performance across domains (not just narrow tasks).

**Trigger sequence:**
1. OpenAI GPT-5 or Anthropic Claude 4 or Google Gemini 3 crosses threshold
2. Global productivity shock: 20-30% white-collar job displacement signals emerge
3. Regulatory crisis: EU invokes AI Act emergency provisions, US Congress emergency hearings
4. China acceleration: CPCNSC orders emergency GPU procurement at any cost
5. US export controls: expanded to cover any chip above certain FLOP threshold globally

**Market cascade:**
- NVIDIA: +100-200% (only path to AGI runs through their chips) then regulatory risk cap
- ASML: +50-80% (every country wants fabs now)
- OpenAI: IPO at $500B-1T valuation
- Anthropic: funding round at $100B+
- White-collar services: −40-60% (consulting, legal, accounting repricing)
- Education sector: structural crisis
- S&P 500: +15-20% then -20% (productivity boom vs displacement crisis)

**Geopolitical cascade:**
- US-China tech war escalates: chip embargo tightened to zero tolerance
- China Taiwan pressure: TSMC becomes even more critical to seize
- NATO activates AI governance framework
- Pentagon: Palantir Maven + autonomous weapons funding 10x
- UN Security Council: emergency session on AI governance (China/Russia veto any framework)

## Branch B — China Achieves AI Parity
*Probability: 30-35% by end 2028*

**Definition:** Chinese frontier models (Huawei Ascend + SMIC + domestic ecosystem) reach 80-90% of US capability for military and industrial applications — despite export controls.

**How it happens:**
- SMIC achieves 5nm-equivalent via multi-patterning (without EUV)
- Huawei Ascend 4.0 achieves H100-class performance per cluster
- China trains models on massive domestic data (1.4B people, unrestricted data access)
- Distillation: Chinese labs train on US model outputs (already happening)

**US response options:**
1. Total technology embargo (all semiconductors, not just advanced) — nuclear option, hurts US companies
2. Accelerated domestic AI investment ($1T+ CHIPS Act 2.0)
3. Allied AI framework (UK/Japan/Korea/EU chip club — lock China out completely)

**Market cascade:**
- NVIDIA: −30 to −40% (China TAM permanently gone + competition)
- AMD: +20-30% (US government "second supplier" mandate)
- US tech companies: split — those with China exposure lose, pure-US gain
- China tech: +50-100% (Alibaba, Baidu, ByteDance — domestic AI ecosystem wins)
- TSMC: ambiguous — US demand rises but China market gone

**Geopolitical cascade:**
- Two AI ecosystems fully formed and incompatible
- Neutral countries forced to choose (India, Saudi Arabia, EU)
- Military AI race: autonomous weapons deployed in proxy conflicts
- Arms control negotiations for AI weapons: likely fail

---

## Branch C — Regulatory Capture / Slowdown
*Probability: 40-45%*

**Pattern:** Regulatory frameworks slow US frontier AI development faster than they constrain China. US companies face EU AI Act compliance, US liability frameworks, copyright litigation. China accelerates in regulatory vacuum.

**Effect:** Neither side achieves decisive breakpoint. Incremental progress continues. Current market structure preserved but with growing China gap closure.

**Market impact:** Current trajectory — NVIDIA remains dominant, gradual sector growth, no systemic shock.
