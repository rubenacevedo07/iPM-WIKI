# Dollar System Stress 2026–2028 Scenarios

*Current USD Hegemony Index: ~72/100 (gradual erosion). The dollar system is not at risk of collapse — but its structural dominance is being slowly undermined by three simultaneous forces: weaponisation, fiscal unsustainability, and deliberate diversification by adversaries.*

---

## Scenario Space

**Trigger nodes:** USD Hegemony Index falls below 65 OR US fiscal credibility event
**Primary actors:** Bessent (fiscal), Powell (monetary), Trump (weaponisation), Xi (diversification)
**Key tension:** Bessent's 3-3-3 fiscal plan vs Trump's tariff/isolation agenda — pulling in opposite directions

---

## Branch A — Gradual Erosion (Baseline)
*Probability: 55-60%*

**The slow-motion de-dollarization:**
- USD reserves share: 58% → 54% by 2028 (−1% per year)
- BRICS+ payment system: operational but marginal (5-10% of intra-BRICS trade)
- China CNY: invoicing growing in Asia/Africa but not global reserve contender
- Gold: central bank accumulation continues — China, Russia, India add 500-800t/year
- Saudi Arabia: accepts CNY for some oil sales (5-10% of China purchases)

**Market impact (slow-burn):**
- USD: modest structural weakness trend (−5-10% DXY over 2 years)
- Gold: structural bid — +$200-400/oz over 2 years
- Bitcoin: gradual Regime C positioning (marginal sovereign diversification)
- US Treasuries: foreign demand slowly declining — yields must rise to clear market
- EM: gradual de-dollarization = less dollar funding vulnerability

**IMP signal updates:**
- USD Hegemony Index: 72 → 68 (2 years)
- Fed Liquidity Pulse: stable (no crisis)
- DSI: gradual deterioration

---

## Branch B — Dollar Crisis (Tail Risk)
*Probability: 10-15%*

**Trigger sequence (requires multiple simultaneous shocks):**
1. US fiscal: bond market strike — 10Y Treasury yield surges past 6.5% (term premium repricing)
2. Fed credibility: Trump fires Powell OR nominates Fed chair who cuts rates despite inflation
3. Geopolitical: major US sanctions action freezes $500B+ of a G20 country's assets
4. Confidence break: foreign central banks accelerate reserve diversification simultaneously

**The cascade:**
- USD: −20 to −30% in 60-90 days (historic move)
- Gold: +$1000-1500/oz (new all-time highs by 3-4x)
- Bitcoin: Regime C fully activates — **existential macro hedge bid**
  - Institutional: BlackRock IBIT inflows surge
  - Sovereign: nation-states begin BTC reserve accumulation
  - Retail: emerging market capital flight (Turkey, Argentina model goes global)
- US Treasuries: yields spike to 7-8%+ (inflation + risk premium)
- S&P 500: −40 to −50% (dollar weakness + rates + growth shock)
- EUR: paradoxical — initially rallies vs USD then ECB stress if EU banks exposed

**IMP signal updates:**
- USD Hegemony Index: 72 → 45 (crisis)
- Bitcoin IMP_CONTEXT: macroRegime → "monetary-crisis", bitcoinBias → "overweight"
- GCRI: 100+ (extreme)
- DSI: critical deterioration

**Why this is tail (not base):**
- Dollar has no credible alternative at scale (EUR=21%, CNY=3% of reserves)
- US military backstop (Pentagon=78/100) remains credible deterrent
- Bessent's 3-3-3 plan is specifically designed to prevent this scenario

---

## Branch C — Dollar Reinvigoration
*Probability: 25-30%*

**Trigger:** Bessent 3-3-3 succeeds + Fed delivers soft landing + energy independence deepens

**The virtuous cycle:**
1. US fiscal deficit: 6.5% GDP → 3% GDP by 2028 (credible consolidation)
2. Fed: successful soft landing — inflation 2% by Q4 2026
3. US energy: shale boom 2.0, LNG exports surge — trade deficit narrows
4. Dollar: structural bid returns — foreign demand for Treasuries recovers
5. USD Hegemony: 72 → 78 (reinvigoration)

**Market impact:**
- USD: +8-12% DXY (structural strength)
- US Treasuries: yields normalise (lower risk premium)
- Gold: consolidates (less safe haven demand)
- Bitcoin: Regime B extended (no monetary stress = no Regime C bid)
- EM: mixed — stronger dollar = EM pressure BUT stable global growth supports EM

## The Trump Paradox

Trump IdeologyProfile: GeoScore=-4.0 (isolationist), AuthScore=7.5 (authoritarian tendency)

The paradox: Trump's policies simultaneously:
- **Strengthen** the dollar short-term (tariffs = inflationary → Fed hawkish → USD up)
- **Weaken** the dollar long-term (weaponisation → adversaries diversify → USD reserve share falls)

Bessent (EconScore=6.0, GeoScore=-1.0) understands this tension. His 3-3-3 plan is an attempt to separate fiscal credibility from the geopolitical weaponisation damage. Whether he succeeds is the central question for Branch B vs C.
