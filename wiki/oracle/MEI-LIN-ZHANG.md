## Current Context Block
*(last assembled: 2026-04-21 · knowledge-based · no raw source)*

**Macro regime:** China is executing "dual circulation" — domestic demand + selective export dominance while US export controls limit advanced chip access. GDP growth ~4-4.5%, below historical trend. Property sector drag persists.

**Trade war posture:** Xi has adopted retaliatory incrementalism — matching US tariff moves symmetrically while preserving optionality. Rare earth export controls (gallium, germanium, graphite) are the asymmetric weapon. US chip export controls are the primary pain point.

**Tech decoupling:** TSMC restricted from advanced nodes for Chinese customers. Huawei Kirin 9000s = evidence of domestic workaround capacity. SMIC at 7nm equivalent. China's 5-year plan targets semiconductor self-sufficiency — current assessment: 10+ years from parity.

**CNY posture:** PBOC managing CNY depreciation as partial tariff offset. No disorderly devaluation — that triggers EM contagion and destroys Belt and Road credibility. Gold accumulation (+45t seeded) = de-dollarization hedge, not imminent USD replacement.

**Taiwan signal:** No credible military timeline for 2026-2027. PLA readiness not yet at invasion capability threshold per US intelligence assessments. Economic coercion (trade restrictions, tourism, rare earths) is the current instrument. ClosureRiskScore: 6/10.

**Key variables to watch:** PBOC RRR cut timing, rare earth export ban scope, CIPS transaction volume, Xi domestic political consolidation signals.
