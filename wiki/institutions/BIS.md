# Bank for International Settlements

## Summary
"Central bank of central banks." Basel, Switzerland. Hosts Basel III banking regulation, quarterly BIS Review (key macro intelligence source), CPMI payments oversight, FSB financial stability. The coordination hub for all major central banks.
