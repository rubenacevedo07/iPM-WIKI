# Wall Street

## Summary
Wall Street is the financial ecosystem node that concentrates the global capital allocation function — equities, credit, derivatives, M&A, IPOs, asset management. It is not a single entity but a network of institutions (JPMorgan, Goldman, BlackRock, Morgan Stanley, Citadel, etc.) whose collective decisions determine the cost of capital for every company and government on the IMP graph. When Wall Street prices risk higher, the whole graph tightens. When it prices risk lower, capital flows into the riskiest nodes first.

## Role and Levers
- **S&P 500 / Nasdaq:** Global equity risk benchmark. Drives wealth effects, pension funding, corporate balance sheets.
- **Credit markets:** Investment grade and high yield spreads determine who can borrow and at what cost.
- **IPO / M&A:** Controls which companies get funded, which get acquired, which get liquidated.
- **Derivatives / vol:** VIX = fear index. Options market positions signal institutional conviction.
- **Repo / money markets:** Short-term funding plumbing. Freeze here = systemic crisis (2008, 2019 repo spike, 2020 COVID).
- **ETFs:** BlackRock iShares, Vanguard, State Street collectively own 20%+ of US equity market. Passive dominance reshapes price discovery.

## Relationship to Fed
Wall Street and the Fed are in a co-dependent relationship that defines the dollar system:
- Fed sets the base rate → Wall Street prices all risk off that rate
- Wall Street dysfunction → Fed intervenes (2008 QE, 2020 SMCCF, 2023 BTFP)
- Fed credibility → Wall Street long-duration asset valuations
- "Fed put" doctrine: market assumes Fed will intervene in severe drawdowns

**Current state (April 2026):** Fed hawkish-hold at 4.5-4.75%. Wall Street pricing 1-2 cuts in 2026. Higher-for-longer = pressure on growth valuations, support for financials. See [[../actors/POWELL-Jerome]].

## Relationship to Pentagon
Wall Street finances the defense industrial base:
- Defense contractor debt issuance (Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop bond markets)
- Pentagon contracts → defense revenue → Wall Street values defense stocks
- War premium: geopolitical escalation → defense sector outperforms
