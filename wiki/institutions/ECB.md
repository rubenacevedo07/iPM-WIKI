# European Central Bank

## Mandate and Tools
Mandate: price stability in the eurozone (20 member states). Target: 2% HICP inflation over medium term. Primary tools: deposit facility rate, main refinancing rate, APP/QE/QT (asset purchase program), TLTRO (targeted lending), TPI (Transmission Protection Instrument — anti-fragmentation tool introduced 2022).

**Unique constraint vs Fed:** The ECB sets one rate for 20 economies with divergent fiscal positions — Germany (surplus) vs Italy/Greece (high debt). This heterogeneity is the ECB's binding structural constraint.

## Internal Structure
- President: Christine Lagarde (term to 2027)
- HQ: Frankfurt, Germany
- Governing Council: 26 members (6 Executive Board + 20 national central bank governors)
- Key voting axis: hawkish North (Germany, Netherlands, Austria) vs dovish South (Italy, Spain, Greece)

## Current Regime
*(Knowledge-based · April 2026)*

**Watchful pause.** Rates held after completing the 2022-2024 hiking cycle (deposit rate peaked ~4%). Small cut in 2025 reversed some tightening. Current stance: slightly restrictive, data-dependent. Inflation ~2% (near target) but growth fragile.

**April 2026 tariff shock:** Asymmetric negative for the eurozone — EU is more trade-dependent than US (~50% export/GDP vs ~12%). ECB now faces a fork: tariffs are theoretically inflationary but practically disinflationary if they crush demand. Lagarde's language has shifted toward "downside growth risk" — cuts back on the table if tariff shock materialises fully.

## Recent Moves
- 2026-Q1: Rates held — maintained "watchful pause" language
- 2026-Q1: TPI (Transmission Protection Instrument) on standby — Italian spread widening monitored
- 2026-Q1: Endorsed EU €100B+ defense spending framework
- 2025: Small cut delivered — signalled normalization without declaring victory on inflation
- 2022-2024: 450bp of rate hikes — fastest tightening cycle in ECB history

## Narrative Shift
Pre-tariff shock: ECB messaging converged with Fed ("higher for longer"). Post-tariff shock (April 2026): ECB diverging from Fed — eurozone growth risk more acute than US. This divergence = EUR/USD bearish signal. See Powell-Lagarde-Divergence comparison.

## Market Impact Channels
- **EUR/USD:** Primary driver of EUR/FX. ECB-Fed rate differential is the mechanical signal.
- **Eurozone sovereign spreads:** BTP-Bund spread (Italy-Germany) = ECB credibility barometer. TPI backstop keeps it below 200bp.
- **European bank equity:** ECB deposit rate = direct margin driver for EU banks.
- **Credit conditions:** TLTRO repayment cycles affect eurozone bank lending → credit → growth.
