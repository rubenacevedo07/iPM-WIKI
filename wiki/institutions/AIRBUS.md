# Airbus

## Mandate and Tools
Europe's aerospace and defense champion. Dual mandate: (1) commercial aviation (A320/A350 family — competes with Boeing), (2) defense/space (Eurofighter, A400M military transport, satellites, missiles via MBDA JV). Listed on Euronext Paris (AIR.PA). Revenue: ~€80B (2025). The clearest embodiment of EU industrial sovereignty — created specifically to prevent full dependence on US aerospace.

## Internal Structure
- CEO: Guillaume Faury
- HQ: Toulouse (commercial), Munich (defense), Madrid (aero structures)
- Major shareholders: French state ~11%, German state ~11%, Spanish state ~4%
- Listed: Euronext Paris / Frankfurt / Madrid (AIR.PA)
- Defense division: Airbus Defence & Space — Eurofighter, satellites, A400M

## Current Regime
*(Knowledge-based · April 2026)*

**Commercial:** Record order backlog (~8,500 aircraft, ~10 years of production). Boeing's quality crises (737 MAX, 787 door plugs) have transferred market share to Airbus structurally. Ramp-up of A320 family is the primary operational challenge — supply chain bottlenecks (CFM/LEAP engines, titanium from Russia alternatives).

**Defense:** Eurofighter demand surging as NATO members rearm. A400M military transport replacing Cold War-era C-130s in EU air forces. Space division: Ariane 6 delays vs SpaceX dominance = existential threat to European launch sovereignty.

**China exposure:** ~20% of commercial backlog is Chinese carriers. China-France/Germany trade tension = Airbus caught in tariff crossfire. Beijing has used Airbus orders as political leverage vs Boeing (reward France for good relations, punish for Taiwan statements).

## Relationships
- JV: MBDA missiles (with BAE, Leonardo, KNDS) — Europe's premier missile consortium
- JV: ATR regional aircraft (with Leonardo)
- Eurofighter: consortium with BAE (UK), Leonardo (Italy), Indra (Spain)
- Competitor: Boeing (commercial), Lockheed/Northrop (defense)
- Customer: All major EU airlines, Lufthansa, Air France-KLM, Iberia + global

## Recent Moves
- 2026-Q1: A320 production rate target 75/month — still below due to engine supply constraints
- 2026-Q1: New Eurofighter orders from Germany, Spain, Saudi Arabia
- 2026-Q1: Ariane 6 launch schedule slipping — SpaceX dominance in commercial launch mounting
- 2025: Titanium supply chain diversified away from Russia — major cost and logistics effort

## Narrative Shift
Airbus has pivoted from "we don't do geopolitics" to explicitly embracing EU strategic autonomy language. Faury is now a regular voice in EU industrial policy debates. The Boeing crisis has been a windfall — but also a warning: Airbus cannot allow the same quality/safety culture to emerge. The defense business is growing faster than commercial for the first time since the Cold War.

## Market Impact Channels
- **AIR.PA:** Bellwether for EU industrial health, transatlantic trade relations, and aerospace cycle. Boeing crises = Airbus rallies. EU-US tariff escalation = Airbus risk (US parts, US customers).
- **EU defense spending:** Eurofighter/A400M orders = direct Airbus revenue. Defense spending surge = multi-year structural tailwind.
- **China orders:** Any deterioration in EU-China relations → risk to ~20% of backlog.
- **Aviation cycle:** A320 deliveries = airline capex indicator, jet fuel demand signal.
