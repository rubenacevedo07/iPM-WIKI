# Pentagon

## Summary
The US Department of Defense — the world's largest single military organisation by budget ($858B in FY2025, ~40% of global military spending). The Pentagon is the coercive power arm of US hegemony: it controls 11 carrier strike groups, 750+ overseas bases across 80 countries, nuclear triad, and the global logistics network that enforces US chokepoint presence. It is simultaneously a customer (defense contractors), a regulator (export controls on dual-use tech), and a geopolitical instrument (forward deployment, deterrence, alliance management).

## Role and Levers
- **Budget:** $858B FY2025 — largest discretionary budget item in US federal spending
- **Forward deployment:** 11 carrier strike groups + 750+ overseas bases = global power projection
- **Export controls:** DoD/BIS joint role in AI chip export controls (NVIDIA, ASML restrictions)
- **Procurement:** Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop, Boeing, General Dynamics — defense industrial base
- **Cyber Command / NSA:** Information warfare, offensive cyber, signals intelligence
- **DARPA:** Technology innovation — GPS, internet, stealth all originated here
- **NATO command:** SACEUR role = de facto NATO military leadership

## Chokepoint Control
The Pentagon's forward deployment map overlaps almost perfectly with the IMP chokepoint map:

| Chokepoint | US Military Presence |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Hormuz | 5th Fleet (Bahrain) + carrier strike group |
| Bab el-Mandeb | Operation Prosperity Guardian, Red Sea naval presence |
| Taiwan Strait | 7th Fleet (Japan) + Taiwan Relations Act commitments |
| Malacca | Singapore logistics hub, PACOM presence |
| Suez | 6th Fleet (Mediterranean) |

The Pentagon does not "control" these chokepoints — it contests them. Its presence is the deterrent that keeps them open. When deterrence fails (Houthis, IRGC), chokepoint risk scores spike.
