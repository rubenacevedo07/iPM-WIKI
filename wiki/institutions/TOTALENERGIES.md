# TotalEnergies

## Mandate and Tools
France's integrated energy major — oil, gas, LNG, renewables, power. Revenue: ~€200B. Production: ~2.5M boe/day. Listed on Euronext Paris (TTE.PA) and NYSE (TTE). One of the "supermajors" alongside ExxonMobil, Shell, BP, Chevron. Unique positioning: more aggressive on LNG and renewables transition than Anglo-American peers, but still heavily oil/gas dependent.

## Internal Structure
- CEO: Patrick Pouyanné — long-serving, close to French state
- HQ: Courbevoie (Paris suburb)
- Rebranded from Total to TotalEnergies 2021 — signalling transition agenda
- Major LNG portfolio: Qatar (QatarEnergy JV), US (Sabine Pass offtake), Papua New Guinea, Mozambique (delayed — security issues), Tanzania

## Current Regime
*(Knowledge-based · April 2026)*

TotalEnergies sits at the intersection of European energy security and the global LNG buildout. Post-Nordstream, Europe's structural shift from Russian pipeline gas to LNG has made TotalEnergies' LNG portfolio strategically critical. The company has maintained Russian operations longer than BP/Shell — controversial but commercially rational. Qatar LNG expansion (North Field) gives TotalEnergies first-mover advantage in the biggest LNG buildout of the decade.

**Renewables:** TotalEnergies has the most credible renewables strategy among supermajors — 35GW+ of solar/wind capacity, integrated power business. Still <10% of revenue but growing.

**Hormuz exposure:** Qatar LNG exports through Strait of Hormuz — any closure = TotalEnergies LNG disruption directly.

## Relationships
- QatarEnergy: Strategic JV partner — North Field LNG expansion (largest in history)
- Iraq / UAE / Saudi Arabia: Upstream production concessions
- Russia: Remaining stakes in Yamal LNG and Arctic LNG 2 (controversial, post-2022 write-downs)
- Renewable power: Grid-scale solar in India, Spain, US

## Recent Moves
- 2026-Q1: Qatar North Field LNG expansion on track — TotalEnergies securing 20-year offtake
- 2026-Q1: Mozambique LNG restart uncertain — Islamist insurgency ongoing in Cabo Delgado
- 2026-Q1: US LNG offtake contracts locked — hedged vs Hormuz risk
- 2025: Wrote down remaining Russia Arctic LNG 2 stake — ~€4B impairment
- 2025: 1GW solar farm India — largest European oil major investment in Indian renewable

## Narrative Shift
The "TotalEnergies" rebrand was meant to signal transition. But the reality is: the company is doubling down on LNG (not phasing out fossil fuels) while simultaneously building renewables. Pouyanné has been publicly skeptical of the IEA's "no new oil fields" narrative — framing LNG as a "transition fuel" for Asia's decarbonization. This puts TotalEnergies in conflict with European green policy but aligned with Asian demand reality.

## Market Impact Channels
- **TTE.PA/TTE:** Tracks Brent with a discount for European political risk premium. LNG spot price exposure. Dividends = €3/share+ (French institutional investors rely on it).
- **European LNG pricing:** TotalEnergies is a price-setter in the European LNG spot market. Qatar shipments → TTF (Dutch gas benchmark) → European energy costs → ECB inflation inputs.
- **Hormuz premium:** Any Iran escalation → TotalEnergies Qatar LNG routes threatened → TTE stock ↓, TTF ↑.
- **Energy transition narrative:** TTE is the benchmark company for "how fast can an oil major transition?" Quarterly capex allocation between oil/gas vs renewables is a signal watched by ESG investors globally.
