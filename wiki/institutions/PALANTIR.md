# Palantir Technologies

## Mandate and Tools

Palantir's stated mandate — codified in CEO Alex Karp's 2025 manifesto *The Technological Republic* — is to provide the West's national security state with AI capabilities sufficient to "scare enemies and, on occasion, kill them." Five core products:

**Gotham** — original intelligence platform, seeded by CIA/In-Q-Tel in 2003. Structured data integration and analysis for intelligence agencies (CIA, NSA, FBI, foreign partners). Pulls structured + unstructured databases to surface relationships, timelines, and patterns at scale. Foundation of the intelligence business.

**Foundry** — enterprise data operating system (2016). Migrates organizational data into Palantir's proprietary ontology, enabling analysis and automation at enterprise scale. Revenue ~40% commercial as of 2025; primary exposure to non-government clients (healthcare, manufacturing, energy).

**AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform)** — launched 2023. Overlays LLM capabilities (GPT-4, Gemini, Claude, and others) on top of Foundry's ontology, enabling AI-driven workflows on proprietary enterprise data without data egress. Primary commercial growth engine. Q1 2026 US commercial revenue +71% YoY.

**Maven Smart System (MSS)** — commercialized Project Maven product. AI platform fusing satellite imagery, drone video, sensor arrays, and SIGINT for automatic object detection, classification, tracking, and targeting. Reduces targeting data transmission from 12 hours (2020) to under 1 minute (2024+). $1.275B DoD IDIQ contract through 2029; 20,000+ users across 35 military entities; adopted by NATO March 2025.

**Skykit / MetaConstellations** — drone AI orchestration layer (Skykit) and multi-sensor fusion framework (MetaConstellations). Integrates drone and satellite constellation feeds into a unified operational picture. Less publicly documented edge of the product stack.

## Internal Structure

- **Alex Karp (CEO):** Co-founder; operational leader since 2003. Chief ideologist and public face in the manifesto era. "Completely anti-woke" (self-described, 2025). Known for unconventional management style.
- **Stephen Cohen (President):** Co-founder; heads commercial operations and partnerships. Lower public profile than Karp.
- **Shyam Sankar (CTO):** Oversees product architecture including Maven. Commissioned as US Army Reserve lieutenant colonel via Detachment 201 (June 2025) — the structural blurring of contractor and military roles made concrete.
- **Peter Thiel (Non-Executive Chairman):** Co-founder; no operational role but retains governance power via Founders Voting Trust / PLTR Holdings LLC. Sold ~$600M in PLTR shares late 2024.

## Current Regime

The Karp doctrine era (2023–present) runs three simultaneous bets:

1. **Ideological boldness:** The manifesto positions Palantir as the explicit ideological vanguard of a West that must choose technical hard power over cultural pluralism. Departure from the earlier "secretive CIA contractor" posture; now openly aggressive in public positioning.
2. **Trump 2.0 contract surge:** January 2025–April 2026, $1.3B+ in US federal contracts across 14+ agencies (DoD, DHS/ICE, Treasury, HHS, State, VA, Energy, Justice, Agriculture, HUD, Commerce, NASA, GSA). The Thiel network's alignment with the Trump administration made this acceleration structurally predictable.
3. **AIP commercial buildout:** Palantir pitching enterprises on AIP as the enterprise AI operating system. If successful, this diversifies away from political contract dependency.

## Relationships

- **Peter Thiel (co-founder, Chairman):** The foundational relationship. Thiel's political network — Vance ($15M Senate donation), Sacks (CSAPT co-chair), former Palantir engineer (HHS CIO) — is the mechanism through which the government footprint expanded. His role is structural, not operational.
- **US DoD / Army (Maven Smart System):** Palantir's largest military relationship. MSS is the DoD's primary AI targeting platform; $1.275B IDIQ through 2029; NATO acquisition March 2025. Detachment 201 embeds Palantir personnel inside the military chain.
- **DHS / ICE (ImmigrationOS, ICM):** Civil enforcement relationship — most politically contested. February 2026 DHS $1B contract makes Palantir the technical backbone of US immigration enforcement.
- **CIA / Intelligence Community (Gotham origin):** In-Q-Tel was among Palantir's first investors (2003). Gotham was designed for IC use. Current IC contract details classified.
- **Ukraine MoD:** Battlefield AI targeting support since 2022. Karp: responsible for "most of the targeting in Ukraine." Detection to target prosecution in under 2–3 minutes. Ukraine is Palantir's AI war laboratory.
- **IDF / Israel (allegation status):** UN Special Rapporteur found "reasonable grounds" that Palantir technology was used in Gaza operations. Palantir has not confirmed a direct IDF contract. Confidence: LOW on direct contract; HIGH on the allegation.
- **NATO:** MSS NATO acquired for Allied Command Operations March 2025 — described as "one of the most expeditious contracts in NATO history."

## Narrative Shift

**Before (2003–2022):** Deliberate obscurity — "secretive government contractor," classified clients, commercial legitimacy questioned. Palantir actively avoided attention to its government work.

**After (2023–present):** The Karp doctrine era inverts this posture. Now openly ideological: the manifesto, the X thread, Karp's "blood money" rebuttal, his description of Palantir's mission as "scaring enemies and killing them." Shift from opacity to aggression appears strategic — political tailwinds under Trump 2.0 make ideological transparency more valuable than reputational cover.
