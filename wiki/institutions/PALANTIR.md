# Palantir Technologies

## Summary
Palantir is the AI/data analytics platform for the US national security state — CIA (Gotham), DoD (Maven), NSA. It is the connective tissue between raw intelligence data and operational decision-making. Palantir AIP is now the commercial AI deployment platform for enterprises. Strategic position: the company that knows the most about how governments make decisions.
