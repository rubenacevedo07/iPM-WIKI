# Volkswagen Group

## Mandate and Tools
Germany's largest industrial company and the world's second-largest automaker (by revenue). Brands: VW, Audi, Porsche, SEAT/Cupra, Škoda, Bentley, Lamborghini, Ducati. Revenue: ~€320B (2025). Employees: ~680,000 globally — Germany's single largest private employer. Listed: Volkswagen AG (VOW3.DE), Porsche AG (P911.DE). Porsche SE (the Piëch/Porsche family holding) controls ~31% of voting shares.

## Internal Structure
- CEO: Oliver Blume (also CEO of Porsche AG — dual role unusual)
- HQ: Wolfsburg, Germany
- China JVs: SAIC-VW and FAW-VW — combined ~3M vehicles/year in China
- Software division: CARIAD — massive investment (~€5B/year), significant delays

## Current Regime
*(Knowledge-based · April 2026)*

VW faces the most acute strategic crisis of any major European industrial company. Three compounding pressures:

1. **China collapse:** VW's China JVs contributed ~40% of group profit 2015-2022. Now BYD, Li Auto, AITO are eating VW's mass-market share in China faster than any prior competitive threat. VW China market share fell from ~20% to ~12% in 3 years. The China profit engine is breaking.

2. **EV transition cost:** VW committed €180B+ over 5 years to EV transition. The problem: EV demand in Europe is below plan (range anxiety + infrastructure + price), and VW's EV software (CARIAD) is 2+ years behind Tesla/BYD. ID.3/ID.4 launched with software so buggy it damaged brand reputation.

3. **Cost structure:** German labor costs (IG Metall union) are 3-4x Chinese competitors. VW announced in 2024 it would close German factories for the first time in its history — triggered massive political and union backlash.

## Relationships
- IG Metall: Powerful union with board representation (German co-determination law)
- German states (Saxony, Lower Saxony): Shareholders + political pressure not to close plants
- SAIC / FAW: China JV partners — strategic dependency on Chinese market access
- Battery suppliers: CATL (Chinese) — VW's primary cell supplier; attempting to reduce dependency via PowerCo battery subsidiary

## Recent Moves
- 2026-Q1: Plant closure negotiations ongoing — IG Metall blocking 3 factory closures
- 2026-Q1: China JV restructuring — seeking to reduce SAIC-VW equity exposure
- 2026-Q1: CARIAD software reorg — external hires from Apple/Google to accelerate
- 2025: Porsche AG IPO proceeds being used to fund EV transition
- 2025: PowerCo (battery subsidiary) construction in Valencia, Canada, Germany

## Narrative Shift
VW has shifted from "we will lead the EV transition" (2021 Diess era) to "we need to survive the EV transition" (Blume era). The company's own internal analysis reportedly shows it cannot compete profitably in EVs at current cost structure vs Chinese peers. The strategic question is existential: does VW become a premium/luxury focused company (Porsche/Audi/Bentley) and cede mass market to BYD? That would mean tens of thousands of German job losses.

## Market Impact Channels
- **VOW3.DE:** Barometer for German industrial health + China consumer sentiment + EU auto tariff policy. China data → VW stock.
- **German GDP:** VW + suppliers = ~5% of German GDP directly. VW restructuring = German recession risk amplifier.
- **EU-China auto tariffs:** EU imposed tariffs on Chinese EVs (2024). China threatened VW in retaliation — politically impossible for Germany to maintain tariffs vs China if VW is threatened.
- **EV commodity demand:** VW battery orders = lithium, cobalt, graphite demand signal.
