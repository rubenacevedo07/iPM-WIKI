# JAPAN

## Strategic Summary
Japan is quietly becoming one of the most strategically important countries in the semiconductor and AI supply chain realignment. TSMC is building major fabs in Kumamoto (Japan). Japanese equipment companies (Tokyo Electron, Shin-Etsu) are critical to global semiconductor manufacturing. Japan joined export controls on advanced chip equipment. Under PM Kishida/successor, Japan has shifted from pacifist security posture to significant rearmament — largest defence budget increase in modern history.
