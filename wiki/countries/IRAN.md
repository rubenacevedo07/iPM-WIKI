# IRAN

## Strategic Summary
Iran controls or can threaten the world's most critical oil chokepoint — the Strait of Hormuz (~20% of global oil supply). Despite comprehensive Western sanctions, Iran has sustained itself through oil exports to China and Russia alignment. The IRGC (Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps) is the primary coercive instrument — controlling Hormuz threat, proxy networks (Houthis, Hezbollah, Iraqi militias), and domestic repression. Nuclear programme is the primary Western leverage point.
