# INDIA

## Strategic Summary
World's most populous country and fastest-growing major economy (~6.5% GDP growth). India is the world's swing voter — maintaining strategic autonomy between US, Russia, and China simultaneously. The largest buyer of discounted Russian oil post-sanctions. A key beneficiary of supply chain diversification away from China (PLI schemes, Apple manufacturing). Modi's vision: make India the next global manufacturing hub while maintaining geopolitical independence.

- Jobless growth — GDP growing but not enough formal employment creation
- Monsoon/food inflation cycle — structural vulnerability
- Religious polarisation — BJP Hindu nationalism vs secular opposition

## External Alignments
Non-alignment 2.0; Quad member (US-Japan-Australia-India); simultaneous Russia oil buyer; China border disputes; BRICS+ member; Israel arms supplier

## Sanctions and Legal Constraints
Not subject to Western sanctions. Actively circumventing Russia oil sanctions by purchasing discounted Urals. Western pressure to reduce Russia purchases intensifying.

## Narrative Trajectory
*(Updated 2026-04-09)*
India positioning as the indispensable swing state. Modi leveraging strategic autonomy to extract maximum benefit from both US (tech partnership, defence deals) and Russia (oil discounts). This non-alignment is increasingly valuable as fragmentation deepens.

## Market Relevance
INR under pressure when USD strong and oil spikes. Sensex/Nifty = domestic growth story. India sovereign bonds = EM risk asset. Key risk: oil import cost (India imports ~85% of oil needs). Key opportunity: PLI-driven manufacturing FDI inflows.
