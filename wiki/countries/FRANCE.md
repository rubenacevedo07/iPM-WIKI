# FRANCE

## Strategic Summary
G7 nuclear power and EU founding member. France uniquely combines NATO membership with strategic autonomy doctrine — the only European state with independent nuclear deterrent. Permanent UN Security Council seat. ECB is Frankfurt-based but Lagarde (French) is President. Macron has positioned France as the diplomatic pivot between US/Russia/China, frequently testing the boundaries of Western consensus.
