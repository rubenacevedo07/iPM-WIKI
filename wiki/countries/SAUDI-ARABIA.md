## Market Relevance
**Oil:** Saudi Aramco produces ~10M bpd — Saudi unilateral production decisions move Brent by $2-5/bbl. OPEC+ cuts coordinated via MBS-Putin axis. Brent price → global inflation → central bank rate paths → all risk assets.

**Petrodollar system:** Saudi oil invoiced in USD → recycled into US Treasuries and USD assets. Petrodollar recycling is a structural pillar of USD hegemony. Any shift to yuan invoicing (already tested with China) = DSI erosion signal. USD-Hegemony-Index input.

**Aramco dividends:** Saudi Aramco ($2T market cap) pays ~$75B/year in dividends — 98% to Saudi government. Aramco cash flow = Vision 2030 funding engine. Oil price < $70 = fiscal deficit territory for Saudi Arabia.

**SAR peg:** Saudi Riyal pegged to USD at 3.75 since 1986. Peg defense requires either oil revenues or FX reserve drawdown. Peg break = catastrophic regional shock (no precedent in modern era).

**Vision 2030 bonds:** Saudi government increasingly active in international bond markets to fund non-oil development. Saudi sovereign credit = implicit oil price derivative.

**Geopolitical premium:** Houthi/Aramco attack risk = instant Brent spike. Abqaiq facility = 5M bpd chokepoint (2019 attack demonstrated $10/bbl reaction).
