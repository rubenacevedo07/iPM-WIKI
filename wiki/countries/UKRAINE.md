## Market Relevance
**Grain/commodities:** Ukraine = 10% of global wheat exports, 15% corn, 50% sunflower oil. Black Sea corridor disruption → EM food inflation (MENA, Africa most exposed). Corridor resumption/disruption = weekly Wheat/Corn futures catalyst.

**Defense spending multiplier:** Ukraine war drove NATO members to 2%+ GDP defense targets. Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop order books at record levels. European defense stocks (KNDS, Rheinmetall, BAE) direct beneficiaries. Defense spending = structural fiscal expansion for EU (breaking EU debt constraints).

**Reconstruction financing:** IMF estimates $400-500B reconstruction cost. Ukraine eurobonds (currently at distressed levels) = eventual reconstruction play. World Bank, EBRD, EU macro-financial assistance programs = credit market signal for EM sovereign debt.

**Frozen Russian assets:** $300B frozen Russian CB assets — potential use as Ukraine reconstruction collateral is active legal/political debate. If precedent set, changes global sovereign asset safe-haven calculus.

**European energy costs:** Ukraine gas transit routes still operational (residual). Full cessation = residual EU gas price pressure. Structural shift to LNG already underway — Ukraine outcome affects pace not direction.

**Palantir exposure:** Palantir's Ukraine AI contract (battlefield intelligence) = live proof-of-concept for defense AI. Ukraine = Palantir's largest non-US government customer.
