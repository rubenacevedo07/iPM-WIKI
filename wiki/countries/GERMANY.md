# GERMANY

## Strategic Summary
Largest EU economy and industrial powerhouse — but structurally dependent on cheap Russian gas (now cut) and Chinese export markets (now contested). The Zeitenwende (2022 strategic turning point) forced Germany to rearm after decades of pacifism. Now facing an existential industrial competitiveness challenge: energy costs 3-4x higher than US, China competing in autos and machinery, defence spending ramping. The engine of Europe is stalling.
