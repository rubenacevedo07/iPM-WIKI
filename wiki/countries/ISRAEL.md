# ISRAEL

## Strategic Summary
Israel is a military and technology power disproportionate to its size. Nuclear ambiguity (undeclared nuclear arsenal estimated 80-400 warheads) is the ultimate deterrence. IDF among the world's most capable forces. Cybersecurity and surveillance technology global exporter (NSO Group, Unit 8200 alumni ecosystem). October 7 2023 Hamas attack and subsequent Gaza war has reshuffled regional alignments — stalled Abraham Accords, strained US relationship, weakened Hezbollah significantly.
