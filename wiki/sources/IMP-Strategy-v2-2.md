# Summary — IMP Master Strategy v2.2

## Source
`raw/IMP_Strategy_v2_2.docx` — Authoritative strategy document. Supersedes v2.0 and v2.1.

## Main Takeaways
- IMP is "the public version of what Bridgewater built privately" — Bloomberg + Palantir capabilities from $19/month
- Built in 5 layers: Knowledge Graph → Social Intelligence → Simulation/Timelines → Personal Workspace → Automation Engine
- The automation engine (Perplexity + Claude + n8n + IMP_ENVELOPE) is what makes the platform self-sustaining
- Oracle tracks 20 fictional machine names covering real analysts, scored weekly with Brier rule
- 17 competitive moats across 5 categories — social network moat is the most compounding
- Exit target: $300M–$1B acquisition by Bloomberg, S&P, Palantir, or Mastercard (Year 7–9)
- Breakeven: 3 Pro users at $49/month. Monthly burn: $1,020.
- The causal graph (1,230+ edges, 18-month build) is the irreplaceable asset

## Key Claims
- EdgeRiskScore = SeverityLevel × BranchProbability, updated every 5 minutes from Polymarket CLOB
- Three-way signal (Polymarket vs AI ensemble vs individual machine) divergence >20pts = alpha
- GCRI replaces keyword-counting GPR with causal propagation through typed entity graph
- Prediction dataset after 24 months becomes licensable fine-tuning data for Bloomberg, Morgan Stanley
- Strategy Lab backtests on graph state — a query that "cannot be run anywhere else on Earth"
