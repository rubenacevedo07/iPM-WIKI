## Microsoft — How He Built It
- **1975** — Microsoft founded with Paul Allen in Albuquerque. First product: Altair BASIC interpreter.
- **1980** — IBM deal: Gates licenses MS-DOS, keeps rights. Bought DOS for $50K from Seattle Computer Products, licensed to IBM with right to sell others. Template for platform monopoly established.
- **1986** — Microsoft IPO at $21/share. Gates a billionaire at 31. 12,000+ Microsoft millionaires created.
- **1990** — Windows 3.0 + Office bundle = corporate standard.
- **1995** — "Internet Tidal Wave" memo pivots Microsoft to web. IE ships free, destroys Netscape.
- **1998** — DoJ antitrust suit. Gates's deposition infamous. Found guilty 2000; break-up order later reversed on appeal.
- **2000** — Gates steps down as CEO; Ballmer era begins.
- **2008** — Full exit from day-to-day role. Ballmer-era Microsoft misses mobile, search, cloud — stagnates.
- **2014** — [[satya-nadella]] CEO. Azure pivot; LinkedIn ($26B 2016), GitHub ($7.5B 2018), Activision ($69B 2023).
- **2023** — Microsoft invests $13B+ in OpenAI for 49% profit share — Gates calls it "the most significant technological development of my lifetime."
- **2026** — Microsoft is now infrastructure layer of AI era — mirror of PC era role.

## BMGF and Global Health
Second-largest WHO funder after US government. $5.5B in 640 grants (2000–2024). More than 50% directed at vaccines.

| Institution | Gates role | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| GAVI — Vaccine Alliance | Co-founded 2000 | 900M+ children vaccinated since 2000; >$4B BMGF |
| CEPI — Epidemic Preparedness | Co-founded 2017 | Funded Moderna, AstraZeneca early |
| WHO | Primary non-state funder | $5.5B across 640 grants |
| Global Fund (HIV/TB/Malaria) | Major donor | >$3B since founding; 50M+ lives saved |

**2015 TED Talk** ("The Next Outbreak? We're Not Ready") — predicted respiratory pandemic 5 years before COVID-19. 45M+ views. Verifiably correct. Now primary fuel for conspiracy theories about his "foreknowledge."

**Structural critique:** When a private foundation provides 6.4% of WHO's total budget over 24 years, directed at programs the foundation also invests in, the conflict-of-interest question is legitimate. BMGF simultaneously funds health programs AND holds equity in pharmaceutical companies through its investment trust.

## Food — Farmland and Agriculture
Largest private US farmland owner: ~275,000 acres. Simultaneously spent billions through Foundation on African agriculture (Green Revolution 2.0) — hybrid seeds, fertilizer access, precision farming. Critics (Navdanya International et al.) argue Foundation approach pushes input-dependent industrial agriculture on smallholder farmers, benefiting agribusiness companies Cascade holds equity in more than farmers.

Gates publicly advocates wealthy nations shift to **100% synthetic beef**. Backed Impossible Foods and Beyond Meat financially. Thesis: current food system incompatible with climate targets; precision fermentation and cellular agriculture cost-competitive by 2030–2035; governments should use carbon pricing to accelerate transition. Investment portfolio positioned accordingly.

Structural question critics raise: the largest private US farmland owner advocates replacing conventional beef — which competes with his own alt-protein investments.

## Philanthropy — The Gates Foundation
BMGF is world's largest private charitable foundation by assets. Founded 2000. $100B spent in first 25 years. 2025 pledge: $200B — virtually all remaining wealth — within 20 years.

**Program areas:**
- **Global Health** — largest program (~$4B/yr of $8.6B total 2024). Polio cases down 99%+ since 1988. Malaria, HIV, TB, maternal health.
- **Global Development** — agriculture, nutrition, financial inclusion. Africa / South Asia smallholder farming. Criticized for undermining food sovereignty.
- **US Program** — K-12 education reform, community colleges. Common Core (Gates funded, controversial).

**[[../actors/BUFFETT-Warren]] partnership** — pledged ~$50B+ to BMGF over time. Gates + Buffett = ~$150B pledged combined. Both co-founded Giving Pledge — 240+ billionaire signatories.

**Structural critique:** A private foundation controlled by one person shapes global health policy, funds WHO priorities, influences regulatory frameworks for vaccines and food, and simultaneously holds equity positions benefiting from those frameworks. Governance problem with no analogue in prior history.
