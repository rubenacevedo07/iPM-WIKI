## Current Assessment
*(Knowledge-based · April 2026 · confidence: low — thin sourcing)*

Macron is the most active European leader in filling the US credibility vacuum created by Trump's transactional approach to NATO. His "strategic autonomy" doctrine — Europe must not be a vassal of either the US or China — has moved from fringe to EU mainstream since 2022. Key levers: France's nuclear deterrent (only EU nuclear power), permanent UN Security Council seat, Airbus/defense industrial base, CAC 40 financial influence. His domestic position is weakened (minority government, far-right ascendant) but his European and international role remains outsized.

## Role and Levers
President of France with decree power under 5th Republic. Commander of France's independent nuclear deterrent (Force de frappe). Drives EU Council agenda, EU defense spending push, Ukraine reconstruction coordination. Macron's Élysée is the primary back-channel between Western powers and any potential Russia negotiation.
