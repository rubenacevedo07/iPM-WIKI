## AI Strategy — The Open Source Bet
Zuckerberg's AI strategy is structurally different from every other major tech CEO. While Microsoft/OpenAI, Google/Gemini, Anthropic build closed proprietary models, **Meta releases frontier models as open source** (Llama 2, 3, 4).

**The logic:** Meta's business is advertising. AI is a means, not an end. Commoditize the AI model layer — make frontier-quality AI free and ubiquitous — and no competitor can charge a premium for AI capabilities, while Meta's true moat (3.3B users + behavioral data) remains intact. Open-sourcing weakens OpenAI and Google's pricing power, builds developer goodwill, costs Meta little since it's not selling AI subscriptions.

**Infrastructure scale:** 350,000+ NVIDIA H100s — largest private cluster globally. Announced data center so large it would cover significant portion of Manhattan, with dedicated energy infrastructure.

**Meta Superintelligence Labs** (announced 2026) — dedicated AGI research division. Poaching researchers from OpenAI, DeepMind, Google Brain. Declared goal: AGI delivered open source.

In the weeks around Trump's inauguration, Zuckerberg executed one of the most dramatic public political repositioning moves in Silicon Valley history. Changes were sweeping, rapid, unmistakably coordinated with the new political order.
