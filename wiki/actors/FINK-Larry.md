## Aladdin — The Systemic Infrastructure
**Asset Liability, Debt, and Derivative Investment Network.** Built since 1988. Most comprehensive financial risk analytics platform ever created. 250M+ calculations per week. Runs Monte Carlo simulations on entire portfolios. Real-time decisions for BlackRock fund managers.

**Licensed to third parties** including competing institutions — BlackRock's risk software manages money for competitors:
- Central banks (multiple sovereign institutions)
- Pension funds (CalPERS, ABP Netherlands, others)
- Sovereign wealth funds (multiple Gulf state SWFs)
- Insurance (Allianz, AXA, MetLife)
- Banks (including competing asset managers)

**The systemic risk paradox:** $21T+ runs through the same risk model. When Aladdin says "sell bonds," thousands of clients may sell simultaneously — creating correlated systemic risk from a tool designed to reduce it. Former Fed officials have called Aladdin a "systemic risk amplifier masquerading as a risk management tool."

Deeper issue: BlackRock holds stakes in the companies whose assets Aladdin analyzes; manages money for institutions that compete with each other; advises governments on policy. Risk models, once adopted universally, become self-fulfilling prophecies — the map becomes the territory.

## Historical Milestones
- **1988** — Founded BlackRock with 7 partners, $5M seed from Blackstone. Year-one AUM $2.7B.
- **1999** — IPO on NYSE (BLK). Valued at $900M at IPO vs $130B+ today.
- **2006** — Acquired Merrill Lynch Investment Management ($9.3B). Doubled AUM overnight.
- **2009** — Acquired Barclays Global Investors ($13.5B) at crisis low. Got iShares — world's largest ETF platform.
- **2008–2009** — Crisis architect. Fed + Treasury called BlackRock to manage $130B+ in distressed assets (Bear Stearns, AIG, Freddie Mac, Fannie Mae).
- **2020** — COVID bailout manager. Fed hired BlackRock without competitive bidding to run $750B bond-buying program while BlackRock held stakes in the bonds' issuers. Congressional investigations triggered.
- **2025** — Trump alignment. Dropped ESG. Exited Net Zero Asset Managers Alliance. Managing $37B US infrastructure fund.
- **2026** — Advising Ukraine reconstruction fund; AI pivot in annual letter warning wealth gap widening.
