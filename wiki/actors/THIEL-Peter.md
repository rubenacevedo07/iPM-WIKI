## Summary

Peter Andreas Thiel (b. October 11, 1967, Frankfurt am Main) is a German-American entrepreneur, venture capitalist, and political patron who constitutes the single most important node connecting Palantir Technologies, JD Vance, the Trump administration, and the Silicon Valley MAGA political alignment. Co-founder of PayPal, Palantir Technologies, and Founders Fund, Thiel built a fortune now estimated at ~$24B (Forbes, 2025) and has spent two decades systematically deploying it to reshape the Republican Party in the image of his idiosyncratic libertarian-nationalist philosophy. He is neither a day-to-day operator nor a visible public figure in the Trump 2.0 era — his influence operates through the network he has built and funded rather than through his own presence.

---

## Role and Levers

**Palantir (non-executive Chairman):** Thiel co-founded Palantir in 2003 with Alex Karp and others, with seed investment from In-Q-Tel (the CIA's venture arm). He has served as Chairman since inception and remains Chairman of the Board as of 2026, though he holds no operational role. His founding-era voting trust (Founders Voting Trust, Rivendell entities, PLTR Holdings LLC) gives him outsized governance influence relative to his economic stake. He sold approximately $600M in PLTR shares in late 2024.

**Founders Fund (General Partner):** The firm, co-founded in 2006 with other PayPal alumni, has backed SpaceX, Airbnb, Lyft, Anduril, Scale AI, and dozens of AI and biotech startups. Several of these companies have since won major Trump-era government contracts, creating a direct return on Thiel's political investment.

**Mithril Capital (co-founder/chair):** Thiel's growth-stage VC fund, the incubator through which JD Vance first entered his network in 2016. Also holds Palantir shares as a vehicle for Thiel's equity.

**Political check-writing:** Thiel has been a Republican megadonor since 2000, calibrating his investments strategically. His most consequential single donation: **$15 million to Vance's 2022 Ohio Senate race** — the largest individual donation to a Senate campaign in US history at the time. He also donated $10M to Blake Masters' Arizona Senate campaign. In 2023, Thiel publicly signaled he would not donate to candidates in 2024, ultimately giving $1.7M — a retreat from his 2022 peak but not an exit.

## Power Profile

**Archetype:** FINANCIAL / POLITICAL hybrid — the "gray cardinal" of Silicon Valley conservatism. Thiel's leverage is not from holding office, making public appearances, or leading a movement. It is from having placed allies (Vance, Sacks, Karp) in positions of state power, and from owning financial stakes in companies that now supply infrastructure to the executive branch.

**Key mechanism:** capital → careers → contracts. Thiel funded Vance; Vance became VP; Palantir's federal contract haul since inauguration exceeds $1.3 billion. Anduril (Founders Fund) won a 10-year Army deal worth potentially $20B. Scale AI (Founders Fund) won new DoD contracts with its managing director embedded in the administration.

## Narrative Shift

- **2000s–2010s:** Libertarian contrarian — funded early internet infrastructure, wrote *Zero to One*, decried technological stagnation, advocated "seasteading" and other exit-from-state experiments. Built Palantir in the post-9/11 intelligence market while maintaining a reputation as an iconoclast.
- **2016:** MAGA pivot — one of the very few Silicon Valley figures to publicly back Trump. Spoke at the Republican National Convention; joined Trump's transition team. Draws criticism for conflict of interest given Palantir's government contracts.
- **2022+:** Kingmaker — the Vance $15M donation cements Thiel as the architect of Silicon Valley-MAGA fusion. Simultaneously steps back from public political profile while his network advances.
- **2025–present:** Off-stage architect. Does not attend White House events or seek formal advisory roles. His allies (Vance as VP, Sacks as CSAPT co-chair, former Palantir engineer as HHS CIO) operate as his proxy network while Thiel retains plausible distance and liquidity.

## Current Assessment (as of April 2026)

Thiel is in a period of deliberate political quiescence. He did not donate significantly in 2024, did not take a formal administration role, and has largely avoided public comment on Trump 2.0 policy. His influence is now structural rather than active — baked into the appointments, contracts, and institutional relationships built during Trump 1.0 and the 2022 political cycle. His primary ongoing mechanism of power is Palantir's expanding government footprint and Founders Fund's position as the de facto investment arm of the national-security tech complex.

**Key unknowns:** Whether Thiel will activate more visibly in the 2026 midterms or 2028 cycle. Whether a future Vance presidential run would draw a large Thiel commitment.

## Key Recent Actions

- **Post-IPO (2020–2021):** Palantir goes public; Thiel retains chairman role; his various holding entities maintain substantial controlling stake via Class F supervoting shares.
- **2022:** $15M Vance donation; $10M Masters donation. Introduces Vance to Trump at Mar-a-Lago.

- **2025–26:** Palantir harvests $1.3B+ in federal contracts under Trump 2.0. Thiel remains chairman. His network runs the administration's tech policy.
