## Current Assessment
As of April 2026, Musk's position is structurally strong but politically isolated. The SpaceX+xAI merger consolidates his AI ambitions on the same balance sheet as his space and satellite businesses, creating a defense-adjacent integrated stack that is difficult for competitors to replicate but also concentrates regulatory and political risk. Wealth remains near all-time highs but is more fragile than the headline number suggests — a forced liquidation event on any single asset would cascade.
