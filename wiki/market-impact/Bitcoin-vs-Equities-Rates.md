# Bitcoin → Markets

## Direct Channels

**ETF flows (institutional on-ramp):**
BlackRock IBIT, Fidelity FBTC, and 8 other US spot ETFs launched January 2024. Combined AUM $50B+.
- Inflow day = institutional demand signal = price support
- Outflow day = institutional distribution = price pressure
- BlackRock IBIT now largest Bitcoin holder after Satoshi-era wallets
- ETF premium/discount to NAV = institutional demand gauge

**Crypto credit:**
- Leverage in futures markets (CME, Binance) amplifies moves 2-3x
- Funding rates (perpetual futures) = sentiment indicator
- When funding rates spike → crowded longs → washout risk
- Bitcoin credit is unregulated — cascades faster than equity margin calls

**Stablecoins:**
- Tether (USDT) $100B+ = unregulated dollar proxy
- Tether redemption risk = systemic crypto contagion
- USDC (Circle) = regulated, backed 1:1, safer
- Stablecoin supply growth = crypto liquidity expansion

## Second-Order Channels

**Tech valuations:**
- Bitcoin ↑ → risk appetite → Nasdaq multiple expansion
- Bitcoin ↓ → risk-off → growth multiple compression
- MicroStrategy (MSTR) = leveraged Bitcoin proxy, included in some indices
- Crypto mining stocks (Marathon, Riot) = high-beta BTC plays

**Liquidity proxies:**
- Bitcoin as real-time M2 proxy: M2 expansion → BTC leads risk assets
- Global liquidity cycle (Crossborder Research) → BTC leads by ~6-12 months
- DXY (dollar index) inverse correlation ~0.7

**EM capital flight:**
- Turkish lira, Argentine peso, Nigerian naira crises → local BTC premium
- Sanctions evasion demand (Russia, Iran) → shadow market bid
- Remittance flows → Lightning Network volume

## Current State — April 2026

**Regime B (Liquidity OFF):**
- Fed held 4.5-4.75%, higher-for-longer
- Real 10Y yield elevated → BTC headwind
- ETF flows: slow/mixed (institutional but not aggressive)
- No macro stress catalyst → no Regime C bid
- BTC range-bound pending Fed pivot signal

**What changes the picture:**
1. Powell signals cut → Regime A → BTC overweight
2. Dollar system stress event → Regime C → BTC macro-hedge bid
3. New geopolitical capital control shock → Regime C → BTC refuge demand
