# Trump Trade War → Markets

*Based on Trump trade policy as of April 2026.*

## Direct Channels
- US tariffs on Chinese goods (Section 301) maintained and expanding
- New investigation March 2026 covering China, EU, and 14 other partners — potential new tariffs summer 2026
- Steel/aluminium tariffs on EU maintained; EV and agriculture disputes active

## Second-Order Channels
- **Supply chain rerouting:** Friendshoring to Mexico, Southeast Asia, India accelerating
- **Inflation:** Tariffs push up imported goods prices — Fed cannot cut as aggressively
- **Tech decoupling:** Export controls on advanced semiconductors tighten → NVIDIA China revenue risk → TSMC capacity shift
- **Retaliation risk:** EU, China retaliatory measures → US exporters hurt

## Affected Countries
- [[../countries/CHINA]] — primary target, export pressure, capital flow
- EU countries — auto, agriculture, industrial goods exposure
- [[../countries/TAIWAN]] — TSMC caught between US export controls and China pressure
- Mexico, Vietnam, India — beneficiaries of supply chain shift

## Affected Commodities
- Semiconductors — export control tightening
- Steel, aluminium — tariffs in place
- Agricultural goods — dispute with EU and China

## Asset-Class Impact
- **USD:** Trade conflict + high Fed rates = USD safe haven bid. But extreme escalation = risk-off globally
- **Equities:** US tech (NVIDIA China exposure), European industrials/autos under pressure
- **Supply chain stocks:** Mexico, Southeast Asian manufacturers benefit
- **Credit:** HY spreads widen on trade uncertainty

## Time Profile
- Short-term: uncertainty premium on global trade, supply chain disruption
- Medium-term: companies completing China+1 diversification strategies
- Structural: geoeconomic fragmentation = permanent efficiency loss but regional bloc formation

## Key Uncertainties
- Do new summer 2026 tariffs materialise?
- Does EU retaliate aggressively?
- How does China respond — currency depreciation? Counter-tariffs?
